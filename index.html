Detailed information regarding our three health departments.

Homoeopathy Department

GENTLE, SAFE, AND SIDE-EFFECT-FREE TREATMENT

Comprehensive homeopathic treatment designed to naturally enhance the body's immune system and completely cure health conditions without any side effects. We provide scientific solutions for chronic allergies, respiratory tract blockages, and lifestyle diseases.

Chronic & Allergic Focus (Allergy)

Allergies, Asthma, Chronic cold, Sore throat, Sinusitis, and Breathing difficulties.

Women's Hormonal Care (Women's Health)

Thyroid fluctuations, Hormonal issues, PCOD, Irregular menstruation, and other gynecological conditions.

Skin & Gastric Care (Skin Diseases)

Psoriasis, Skin rashes, Dandruff, Acne, Digestive disorders, Acidity, Stomach ulcers, and Piles.

Lifestyle Diseases (Lifestyle)

Diabetes, Blood pressure, Uric acid variations, Arthritis, Joint pain, and Cholesterol control.

Physiotherapy Department

SCIENTIFIC PHYSICAL RESTORATION & REHABILITATION

Physiotherapy is a treatment method that helps reduce pain, restore mobility, and improve bodily functions through scientific exercises and electrotherapy. At Carmel Holistic Centre, Adimaly, unmatched treatments are available in the following divisions under the direct supervision of experienced sisters and professional therapists.

Ortho Physiotherapy (Ortho)

Joint pain, Back pain, Neck pain, Periarthritis shoulder (PA shoulder), Disc-related disorders, Knee pain, Heel pain, Tennis elbow, other musculoskeletal disorders, and post-operative stiffness.

Neuro Physiotherapy (Neuro)

Stroke, Parkinson's disease, Facial Palsy (Bell's Palsy), Spinal cord injuries, Head injuries, and Numbness in limbs.

Pediatric Care (Children's Division)

Cerebral Palsy, Erb's Palsy, Autism, and congenital physical disabilities Assessment & Therapy.

Sports & Women Rehab (Women's)

Ligament injuries, Bruises, Muscle strains, Antenatal/Postnatal exercises, Urinary incontinence management, and Pelvic floor strengthening.

Counselling Services
